Get to know me

I'm Juan Canseco, a software developer with professional experience in Java/Android and REST API integration. In my current IT Assistant role at CADISA, I support a fuel volume monitoring platform, investigating and resolving issues through SQL Server queries, stored procedures, and log analysis. My experience as a Service Desk Team Leader has also involved coordinating teams and communicating with clients during critical incidents.

In my projects, I build solutions with C#/.NET, Java/Spring Boot, and Angular/TypeScript, using JWT authentication, SQL databases, Redis, and automated tests. I apply Clean Architecture, Vertical Slice, and CQRS-style flows to organize responsibilities and support maintainability. I am open to learning new languages and frameworks, bringing this foundation to the technologies each team and project needs.
